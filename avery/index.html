Avery's Toolbelt

A calm, hands-on cartoon for children aged 3 to 6. Avery, who is five, finds out how real things work - a 1937 car in the garage, a kitchen, a house, and the books on her shelf - with her family, her little brother Gus, her friend Kai and their dog Spec. A grown-up's hands are always on anything hot, sharp or moving.

Avery Uploader is the small private tool the show's creator uses to put new episodes on the show's own YouTube channels. It is not a public app and has no other users.
