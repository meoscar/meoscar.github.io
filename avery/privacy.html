Privacy Policy - Avery Uploader

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Avery Uploader is a private tool used only by the creator of Avery's Toolbelt to upload the show's own episodes to the show's own YouTube channels.

What it uses

With the channel owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and mark them as made for kids. The permission it holds is stored privately and used for nothing else.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share any information about viewers or any other person.
  • It has no users other than the channel owner, and no sign-up.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

Children

The show is made for children, and every episode is uploaded as "made for kids". The tool never collects anything from or about a child.

Google API Services

Avery Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The owner can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
